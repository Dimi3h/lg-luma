A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs, styled after the Apple TV interface.

  • One screen with your widgets above a grid of every app in your own order (move apps from the long-press menu or drag them with the Magic Remote), and small icons for the app store and Settings; hidden apps are listed in Settings.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, choose a night wallpaper, toggle gradient shading, enable automatic background rotation, and switch between dark, light or automatic appearance.
  • A minimal clock screensaver on black after 1 to 30 minutes, moving against burn-in.
  • Optionally show HDMI inputs as small icons in the top bar, with custom names, visibility and live cable and signal status.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Personalize with greeting, date, clock, and weather.
  • Instant, motion-free card focus with a light highlight; Reduced motion for the rest.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Dutch and English.
