A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs, styled after the Apple TV interface.

  • One screen with your widgets above a grid of every app, pinned favorites first, plus Search and Settings cards.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, toggle gradient shading, and enable automatic background rotation.
  • Optionally show HDMI inputs as cards above the apps, with custom names, visibility and live cable and signal status.
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Personalize with greeting, date, clock, and weather.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Czech, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
