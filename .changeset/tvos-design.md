Restyle the launcher after current Apple tvOS (tvOS 26/27, Liquid Glass) and turn it into one tvOS-style main screen. Features and preferences are kept; inputs move behind a preference.

- **One screen**: the Home/Apps/Settings tab bar and the TV-settings button are gone. Beneath the clock and the date, greeting and weather (the top shelf) is one six-column grid of every app: your pinned favourites in their saved order, then every other app by title. Two small icons at the top right open LG's app store (**Apps**, moved out of the grid) and **Settings** (Back returns to its icon); without the store app only Settings shows. Pinning moves an app into the favourites block; hold OK on a favourite to reorder it.
- **Compact shelf and scrolling**: the date shares the clock's line, with the greeting and one row of weather beneath, and three rows of cards (18 cards) fit without scrolling. One margin sets the rhythm: the clock starts as far from the top as the third row ends from the bottom, with 76 px between rows. With more rows, the grid scrolls by whole rows past the third and the shelf collapses; returning to the first row brings it back. Up/Down follow the grid's six columns, including offscreen rows.
- **Launching and options**: OK launches the focused app, as on tvOS. Menu, or holding OK on an app that is not a favourite, opens its options.
- **No app search**: there is no Search card or app search keyboard (the keyboard stays for the weather location and other text fields). Hidden apps are listed in **Settings › General › Hidden apps**, where OK shows one again; the list has an empty state when nothing is hidden. Category filters were removed.
- **Inputs**: HDMI inputs no longer appear on the main screen by default. **Settings › Widgets › Inputs › Show widget** adds them as small glyphs with a status dot in the top bar, left of the icons: focus names the input and its status, OK switches to it and a long OK opens its settings. The remote's input button and the Inputs settings stay as before.
- **App names**: one On/Off setting in General; titles appear only beneath the focused card.
- **Focus and motion**: focus is static on app cards, wallpaper cards and throughout Settings: it changes in the first frame after the key with no lift, scale, spring, tilt or fade. A focused card is a few percent brighter with a light 2.5 px rim and its title beneath; in Settings the focused sidebar entry or row turns opaque white with its shadow at once, keeps that look while OK is held, and a toggle shows its new value in the same frame. Settings lists scroll in about 120 ms, sections and subpages switch without a fade, and every Settings section is prepared while idle so its text is ready. The top-bar icons and popups keep a short lift (about 120 ms for the icons); the main grid scrolls and pages fade in about 200–250 ms. Reduced motion snaps all of it, and hit regions never move. The former Focus tilt preference is gone; saved preference files that carry it still load.
- **Night wallpaper** (off by default): a background's options offer **Use at night**; between sunset and sunrise at the weather location (21:00–07:00 without one) it replaces the chosen wallpaper.
- **Linear keyboard**: the weather location is entered on a tvOS-style single-row keyboard (also on the TV, instead of the system keyboard) with live location suggestions beneath it; OK on a suggestion saves it.
- **Text size** (Settings › General): Default or Larger (about 1.15× body text; titles unchanged), with the three-row main screen still fitting.
- **Jiggle**: while you move a favourite, all favourites wobble slightly, as tvOS icons do; none under Reduced motion.
- **Appearance** (Settings › General): Dark (default, unchanged), Light (lighter wallpaper and glass, dark text) or Automatic, which switches at sunrise and sunset at the weather location (07:00–21:00 without one). Forecasts now include today's sunrise and sunset.
- **Screensaver** (Settings › General, 10 minutes by default; Off, 1, 5, 15 or 30 also): a slow Ken Burns over the bundled and uploaded wallpapers with an optional clock, only while Luma is in front; any key or pointer movement ends it. Still pictures under Reduced motion.
- **Wallpaper dim**: the main screen dims its wallpaper by a constant amount behind the shelf and grid for legibility; it never animates or reacts to the focused app.
- **Favorite badges** (Settings › General, off by default): the heart on pinned favourites is now optional.
- **Text**: one text path for every TV: Inter glyphs are rasterised on the CPU with exact 256-level coverage on a background thread and drawn from a bounded glyph atlas, one pixel-snapped quad per glyph, with light text slightly boosted for sRGB blending. Overlapping contours (Inter's stems and arches) fill as one shape, so letters such as n, m and e have no seams or spikes. The Settings › System › Text rendering choice is gone; a saved choice still loads. Vector logos and icons request 4× MSAA again and fall back to their one-pixel edge ramp only when the GPU grants no samples. Luma writes its startup facts (MSAA samples obtained, the first frame's glyph atlas fill, drawable and GL strings) to `luma-diagnostics.json` next to its preferences.
- **Typography and corners**: Inter Display (from the bundled Inter's optical-size axis) for large titles, lighter tracking, the date in sentence case, and continuous (superellipse) corners. Resting cards have no rim; it appears with focus.
- **Layout and type** follow the tvOS guidelines: 80 × 60 safe area, 260 × 156 cards with 40/76 spacing, and the tvOS text styles from Title 2 down to Caption 2, with nothing smaller.
- **Liquid Glass-style panels** (a lighter, more saturated blurred background, a soft top highlight and a thin bright top edge) for the top-bar icons, the settings sidebar and rows, context menus, alerts, keyboard and notices. Bundled backgrounds and new uploads get a small blurred copy for this; older uploads use a translucent fallback.
- **Settings**: a glass sidebar with a title, rounded rows, and the section and subpage path as a title line above the content. Long lists fade out above the bottom safe-area line and scroll so the focused and last rows stay inside it.
- **Overlays**: the options popup becomes a context menu beside the focused card, deletion a centred alert with side-by-side buttons, notices a compact glass pill (Caption 1) that fades in and rises a few pixels just above the bottom safe-area line, each replacing the last, and Recent apps a single row of cards with the focused one titled.

- **Dutch clock and date**: Dutch defaults to a 24-hour clock until a format is chosen in Settings (an explicit choice is kept), and the date after the clock is lowercase as in Dutch ("maandag 9 november").
- **Languages**: this fork ships English and Dutch only; a saved choice of another language loads as English. The Noto Chinese and Korean fallback fonts are gone with them, which makes the IPK about 28 MB smaller; characters Inter lacks draw as its missing-glyph box. The Dutch strings were reviewed by hand rather than machine-translated.
- **Updates** come only from the `Dimi3h/lg-luma` fork's releases and Homebrew feed, never from upstream; until the fork publishes its first release the check quietly finds nothing newer.
