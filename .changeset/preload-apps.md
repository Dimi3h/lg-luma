Preload apps (Settings › General, on by default): while Luma is in front and idle, it starts up to three of your most used web apps hidden so they open at once, only when enough memory and swap are free and fewer than three of your apps already run, and turns off LG's own preloads of apps you hid in Luma (and back on when you show them again or switch the setting off).
